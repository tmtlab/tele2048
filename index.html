How to Play

Basic Rules

Use arrow keys or swipe to move tiles. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one!

Power-Ups

  • 🔀 Shuffle: Rearrange tiles
  • 🔨 Hammer: Remove any tile
  • ✨ 2x Score: Double points for 30s
  • 🛡️ Shield: Protect a number from bombs - follows the number!

Coins & Stars

Earn coins by merging tiles. Use coins to buy power-ups. Buy more coins with Telegram Stars!
